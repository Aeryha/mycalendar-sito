2. What the service is

MyCalendar is an Android application that lets you manage a calendar synchronised with Google Calendar, with custom event types and counters, notes, scheduled messages, voice entry of events, and a home screen widget.

The app runs entirely on your device and communicates directly with the Google services you authorise. There is no server operated by the developer, and consequently no online service is being supplied: what you receive is the program, not a hosted service.

  • An internet connection in order to synchronise. The app also works offline by queueing your changes, but without a network it cannot bring anything into step.
  • For voice entry: microphone permission and a one-time download of the language model (around one gigabyte). Both are optional, and the app remains usable without them.

7. Limits of reliability — please read this section

This is the most important section on the page, and it is not boilerplate.

Reminders, alarms and scheduled messages rely on Android's notification and alarm systems. On many devices those systems may delay or suspend an application's notifications for reasons no developer can neutralise: battery optimisation, power-saving modes, the app being force-stopped, the phone restarting, or modifications introduced by the device manufacturer.

It follows that:

  • you should not rely on MyCalendar as your only safeguard for a shift, a deadline, a medication or any commitment whose consequences you could not bear;
  • for anything critical, keep a second, independent reminder;

  • the backup to Google Drive is a convenience, not a guaranteed archive. If some data is indispensable to you, keep a copy of your own.
